Dynamic Diagnostics keeps the diligence evidence live: what moved this week, why, and what to do about it

The same source systems the pack was built from, read again every week. Each series is tested against its threshold, and anything that breaches gets a written diagnosis and a next step. You hear about drift in week three, instead of at the quarterly certificate.

20+ monitors on every board across revenue, pricing, inventory, acquisition, retention, concentration and covenant headroom, each with a registered threshold and read every week

For lenders and sponsorsCovenant headroom and early warning on every portfolio company, read from the same systems the underwriting used, with a diagnosis attached and a named owner before anything changes.
For CEOsThe same board on your own business. What moved this week, why, and what to change, before your lender or your board asks the question.
